## Decision

Each mechanism declares required and optional inputs at the decision time,
their canonical source, freshness, missing-value handling, expected semantic
effect, causal receipt, deterministic replay rule, and fallback. A field that
exists only after the decision cannot be used as a decision feature.

The readiness lifecycle is:

```text
input_precheck -> implementation_validated -> activation_ready
```

The only statuses are:

- `deterministic_ready`: required inputs, symbolic boundary, behavior,
  determinism, disconnect evidence, and causal receipts pass;
- `evidence_only`: read-only diagnostic calculation is allowed, but no
  canonical decision may change;
- `unavailable`: required contract or implementation is absent, and the
  explicit baseline is used.

## Mechanism contracts

| Mechanism | Required decision-time inputs | Required effect and receipt |
|---|---|---|
| LoopX | private goal/todo/claim/history, quota view, current continuation boundary, Zyra identity refs | continuation scope, claim/quota behavior, validation-before-spend, restart receipt |
| ARG | task phase, current requirement revision, unresolved obligations, capability registry, immutable graph, continuity verdict | joint role-node-incident-edge base proposal with coverage, reachability, communication, and switch-cost terms |
| CARD | ARG base topology plus health, load, latency, cost, privacy, location, freshness, and confidence | directional residual add/drop/reweight diff with hysteresis and switch-cost reason |
| AgentPrune | spatial/temporal edges, delivery, bytes/tokens/cost, redundancy, evidence use, critical paths, verifier outcome | deterministic keep/drop set, protected edges, savings, and outcome receipt |
| MaAS | committed topology, operator catalog, permission, capability, health, cost/latency, budget, obligations, verifier contract | concrete operator/breadth/depth candidates, scheduler input, and verifier-gated early-exit decision |

All mechanisms require a complete causal chain from input snapshot through
proposal, symbolic verdict, commit or route, physical execution, artifact, and
verification/outcome. Missing links may support diagnosis but cannot prove
production effect.

## Determinism

The same canonical snapshot and config digest must reproduce the same
proposal, projected delta, selection, and receipt. Any nondeterministic
pretrained output is first frozen as a read-only observation with a digest or
is excluded from default decisions.

## No-policy-training

Phase 2 has no training, fine-tuning, online learning, reinforcement learning,
policy gradient, textual gradient, training dataset, training checkpoint, or
mutable learned parameter lifecycle. Raw evidence volume is not a training
sample count. Input gaps are closed with instrumentation and receipts, a
narrower deterministic scope, or a lower readiness status.
