to_unstacked_dataset is broken for single-dimension variables.

A `DataArray` with a single dimension, when stacked with `to_stacked_array` and then unstacked with `to_unstacked_dataset`, raises `MergeError: conflicting values for variable 'y' on objects to be combined` instead of producing a working roundtrip.

Minimal reproduction:
    arr = xr.DataArray(np.arange(3), coords=[("x", [0, 1, 2])])
    data = xr.Dataset({"a": arr, "b": arr})
    stacked = data.to_stacked_array("y", sample_dims=["x"])
    unstacked = stacked.to_unstacked_dataset("y")

Expected behavior: a working roundtrip for single-dimension variables, matching the already-working multi-dimension case. Preserve unrelated stacking/unstacking and combine behavior.
